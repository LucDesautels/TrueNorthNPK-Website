[G] True North NPK is a three-piece band - Noel Desautels, Paul Cusenza and Kevin Zarnett. The "NPK" is simply the three of them: friends who write and record songs together.

[H] Their music leans on folk and rock roots, with honest lyrics about resilience, gratitude, family and finding the sun-lit route through hard times. Their debut album, Titanium Attitude, was produced by the band, with artwork in the style of West Coast art.
